The Almanac is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and how it is used.

What data we collect

When you subscribe to our newsletter or oracle mailing list, we collect your email address and, optionally, your first name. This data is collected solely through forms on this website and stored via MailerLite (our email marketing provider).

How we use your data

Your data is used exclusively to send you emails from The Almanac — new work, offerings, and transmissions — based on your explicit consent at the time of subscription. We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party, except MailerLite as our processor.

How long we keep your data

We retain your data for as long as you remain subscribed. You may unsubscribe at any time via the link included in every email, at which point your data is removed from our active list.

Your rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at thealmanac.elisa@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

This website uses cookies solely through MailerLite for the purpose of managing newsletter subscriptions. No analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies are used.

Changes to this policy
