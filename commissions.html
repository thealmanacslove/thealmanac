Soul Portrait Commissions

Art to feel yourself breathing.

What is a soul portrait

A soul portrait is an intuitively created piece of art tailored to your energetic imprint. Each piece guided by the Almanac's sensitivity. Colours, shapes, materials — all holding a specific encryption and chosen for your exact frequency. Anchoring the colours of your soul, illuminating the movements of your breath, expressed freely.

Like a dancer moving with the music, the Almanac dances with your energy.

Inquiring for a custom piece is already starting the process of creating more beauty in your life — by following this nudge of your desire.

Open the process

The Almanac listens — to a person, a union, a home, a desire

Define your desire: a soul portrait of yourself, to celebrate your journey past and to come. A portrait of your union, existing or incoming — celebrating love and life. A portrait for your home or your business, liberating the potential at the core.

The process

The Inquiry

The Journey

Working with the lunar cycle, the process of creation takes at least 4 weeks for the Almanac to feel into the energy of the piece and choose the right moment of creation. You'll be included in each step of the process through a private communication channel.

Once created, the piece generally needs to breathe a little before being shipped securely to you.
